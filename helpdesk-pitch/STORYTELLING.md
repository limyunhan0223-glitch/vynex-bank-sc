# Visual storytelling direction

Creative revision: 55% executive presentation, 25% custom vector illustration, 15% enterprise UI/data motion, 5% cinematic polish.

## Visual family

Keep the existing navy environment, typography, information hierarchy and functional accent colors. Custom SVG assets use 2-unit rounded strokes on a 64-unit grid, consistent rounded corners, translucent tinted fills and one restrained shadow direction. The full-size support workstation uses the same palette with shallow desk/monitor depth, a professional jacket, headset and minimal facial detail.

`src/components/VectorObjects.tsx` contains reusable email, chat, telephone, headset, laptop, ticket, document, manual, knowledge, search, AI, database, API, shield, workflow, checkmark, warning, customer, support-agent, L2-engineer and SME-reviewer objects. Character variants share one drawing system.

`src/components/StoryMotion.tsx` owns connector travel and notification pulses. Movement follows actual cubic connector paths. All timing derives from the Remotion frame. No random motion, external illustrations, emoji, CSS animation or timers.

## Scene 01 revision

The central card becomes an animated agent/workstation. Email creates a notification, chat arrives through a separate curve, and telephone rings with two restrained pulses. Messages enter the operator's inbox. The monitor visibly interprets a message, scans documents, runs diagnostic steps, populates ticket fields, applies a category tag, moves a ticket to a queue, then hands it to an L2 engineer.

Knowledge sources slide in progressively and send information back toward the workstation. Folded documents, open manuals, stacked tickets, a resolved status and a reviewer portrait distinguish the sources. Additional low-contrast connector paths and staggered enquiries build the manual burden. One scene remains legible at every stage.

Secondary movement freezes at the hero setup, then the original hero type treatment takes priority. In the final 36 frames, the channel endpoints and scattered knowledge paths start converging at one cyan point. Scene 02 is not built and no AI system is revealed.

The master audio and all scene boundaries are unchanged. The source does not narrate classify, route and escalate as separate words; the three micro-actions remain inside the existing “deciding where each case should go” beat. The workstation sequence illustrates manual handling, not a claim that every enquiry is escalated or that the current operation is automated.

## Future scenes — direction only

- Knowledge: move documents through preparation stages and visibly change their state.
- Controlled learning: stop a resolved conversation at the governance gate, then send candidate knowledge through SME review.
- Grounded AI: search approved evidence; stop an unsupported answer and reveal clarify/escalate paths.
- Ticketing: move a case packet through the approved API and populate the existing ticket.
- Escalation: assemble earlier observations into a handover package for the recurring L2 engineer.
- Governance: stop unauthorized actions at visible boundaries; make permitted paths calm and clear.
- Roadmap: activate milestones progressively along the implementation journey.

These are implementation guidelines only. Scenes 02–12 remain unbuilt pending Scene 01 creative approval.
